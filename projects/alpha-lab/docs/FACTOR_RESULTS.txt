Executable account reconstruction
---------------------------------

V8 implements signed raw-share terminal-action accounting and two sparse,
reduce-only priority queues.  Every current-session terminal parent is
permanently restricted before voluntary order execution, including a parent
the account did not hold.  Stale action and risk exits are canceled.  Action
recipients exit first; held names without frozen risk support exit second; an
ordinary buffered or monthly batch follows only when the same security was not
already due at that close.  Queued risk exits stay projected to zero and
ineligible for alpha even if support later returns.  Published action and risk
exit rows must reconcile to an earlier journal event and to reconstructed
pre-trade holdings.  V8 contains zero trade-vector rows for a terminal parent on
or after its terminal session in all three scenarios.

The strict account is censored for 5,812 sessions and ends with 26 unresolved
terminal names, so it has no final observed NAV or full-path return.  The two
imputation sensitivities have 12 and 15 unresolved sessions and finish with no
unresolved terminal names or raw claims.  Their final observed NAV estimates
are $1,125,440.14 and $1,133,008.82 from a $1,000,000 start, but
complete_path_return remains null because their histories contain unresolved
gaps.  These endpoints are path-dependent sensitivity diagnostics, not valid
strategy performance or bounds.

The accepted replay contains 150/199/207 stock claims created across the three
scenarios, 150/197/204 deliveries and action-recipient exit rows, and
180/713/748 risk-orphan exit rows.  The event-count changes from V7 follow from
the permanent terminal restriction and the resulting 23-year optimizer path;
they are not a new signal or model selection.  Forward factor output is
unchanged from the frozen parent/action methodology.  Registered action
journal, daily, forward and trade-vector artifacts are byte-identical to the
isolated engineering reproduction.  Monthly records differ only in
solver_seconds.  Outcomes differ by one summary-only value at two ULP; daily,
trade and event paths are unchanged.

Remaining data work
-------------------

Exact JKP replication still requires the stock-level source inputs and formula
history not present in current providers.  Historical issuer identity,
industry membership and action-known timing remain point-in-time unattested.
Historical stock-borrow availability and realized borrow fees remain absent.
Those data issues, and the 36 blocked characteristics, should be addressed in
new immutable input bundles before any new strategy trial.  The untouched
2023-2025 period must remain reserved until a separately approved final model
and complete executable-account methodology are frozen.
