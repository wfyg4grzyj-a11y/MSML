P3 accounting conventions
-------------------------
methodology = raw_share_cash_action_ledger_v1
Inputs are unadjusted USD/share closing prices and explicit share/action terms.
Adjusted-price total returns are restricted to labels/diagnostics and are never
also credited as ledger returns. Quote basis and source history are declared in
strict typed inputs. A source declaration is not independent historical proof.

An initial session establishes the opening marked account. Economic actions
start on later sessions. Every modeled XNYS session, including early closes,
must be present with its exact UTC close. Information times normalize to UTC.
Decision inputs must be known by the decision close. A decision fixes target
shares; fills occur at least one later session close. Fill-day prices cannot
redesign earlier quantities. Splits adjust pending decision-share targets.

Each session: accrue the previous interval's cash/funding/borrow; process splits
and prior-close action entitlements, then scheduled payments/delivery; update
raw marks; assess and fill the scheduled batch; record balances and actual
risk/constraint coverage; form new decision-close orders. Orders have day time
in force, partial remainders expire, and a later decision cancels/replaces a
pending same-security target, including when the replacement is right-censored.

Fills use raw close plus signed explicit slippage and absolute-notional
commission. Participation limits use that session's supplied share volume. A
research order can contain fractional shares; lot rounding and cash-in-lieu
broker rules are not inferred. Arithmetic uses Python/NumPy/SciPy float64 under
the versioned methodology, with seed and fixed threads in the resolved spec.
A quote can mark a halt without being tradable. Missing quotes never execute at a
stale mark. A deficient locate or partial leg can reject an entire integrated
batch if the remaining fills would breach its declared constraints; no hidden
resizing, liquidation, relaxation or repaired neutral portfolio is inserted.
Reductions of existing exposure can retain named pre-existing violations, which
remain visible. Cash-debit restrictions still apply. This is a research fill
model, not an exchange/broker execution or market-impact model.

Account state contains USD cash, raw shares and signed cash/stock entitlements.
Short proceeds are offset by the marked short liability. Short proceeds and
pending signed liabilities are reserved from interest-bearing cash; no rebate
is assumed. T0 research settlement is an explicit simplification, not broker
settlement. Credit/debit rates are scenario inputs. ACT365F or ACT360 accrues
actual intervening calendar days on prior-close balances, including weekends.

Dividends use shares held before ex-session orders, including signed short
payables. Payment moves a claim to cash without another profit. Splits adjust
shares and marks inversely. Cash, stock and mixed mergers require a single
verified package with per-share USD cash, stock ratio/recipient and scheduled
payment/delivery; no market-cap amount is interpreted as per-share proceeds.
Stock consideration remains a signed claim until delivery. An unknown nonterminal
distribution retains the parent shares with an unpriced entitlement and does
not assert a delisting. Recipient splits
adjust outstanding share claims. Unsettled dividend rights and financing of
undelivered short-stock claims remain unpriced when their terms are unknown.
Payment dates within the supplied window must be sessions: weekend cash flows
cannot be silently approximated. Extend the event model with separate timing
evidence before claiming actual non-session cash accrual.

Loan observations record when known, expiry, available shares, annual fee and
evidence reference. The latest known observation controls; expiration never
falls back to an older observation. New shorts need decision-known availability
covering the fill and a current fill-time check. Recalls/expiry can block fills
without changing decision quantities. Missing held-position fees remain unpriced
and suppress observed NAV/returns; closing the position does not erase an old
unpriced cost. The explicit_hypothetical policy instead uses a deliberately
declared fee/capacity assumption and remains provisional. FINRA short interest
is not lending evidence.

Stale raw marks are retained only as provisional valuation estimates, with
unresolved quantities visible. A never-marked received security has no numeric
NAV estimate; known_components_nav_usd is only a subtotal, not an assumed zero
liquidation. Economic terminal uncertainty never creates cash proceeds. Quote
recovery can restore an endpoint valuation; no gap-spanning price move becomes
a one-day observed return. A missing prior/current valuation or cumulative
unpriced cost suppresses observed daily returns.

NAV changes reconcile to cash interest, funding, borrow, action changes, raw
mark changes and signed trade costs at an explicit USD tolerance. Component
changes during missing valuations explain known subtotals only; they are not
fully observed economic P&L. Producer audit completion, actual valuation coverage
and economic resolution are reported separately. False input attestations are
never upgraded by a successful calculation. investment_ready remains false.

Risk boundary and portfolios
----------------------------
risk_adapter.py reads the sibling equity-risk Forecast.save() format:
forecast.json and forecast.npz. It does not import or modify the sibling project.
Pin the files with an explicit RiskBinding: interface-document hash, source
policy, benchmark/cash/calendar IDs, stable share-class namespace/order, training
cutoff, availability time/kind/evidence, source history and producer attestations.

The adapter checks numeric arrays without pickle, covariance shape/symmetry/PSD,
units=squared_decimal_return and target=additive_excess_returns. Consumer use
specifies the exact security order, horizon and maximum age. Wrong/extra/missing
held names, units, versions, stale/future availability or future calibration fail.
There is no implicit reorder, horizon scaling or conversion to compounded total
return uncertainty. Real artifacts require matching producer data_provenance,
PIT/audit attestations, calibration/selection cutoff and archived availability.
Those attestations remain producer responsibilities; hashes preserve their
claim but do not prove unavailable historical facts. Synthetic artifacts can
only be used as synthetic_validation, never promoted to attested_research.
An explicit one-session diagonal variance baseline supports software testing
while real risk evidence remains unavailable; it is not a fitted equity model.

The bounded reference projection uses unitless scores, not expected-return
forecasts. A net/beta projection supplies a target, then a constrained quadratic
objective balances distance to that target and a declared covariance penalty.
Constraints cover actual gross/net/beta/name, confirmed issuer gross, sector net,
turnover, eligibility, tradability and short capacity. Absolute-value epigraphs
enforce gross and turnover without approximate signs. Source coverage and held
coverage differ; ineligible/untradable positions are retained in inputs and
checked, not erased. Missing issuer/sector/beta/valuation evidence fails when
the selected policy needs it. Incumbent shorts without a current locate remain
visible and cannot increase. No automatic fallback or parameter relaxation.

Solver limits, iterations and numerical tolerances are explicit. The reference
implementation caps active/frozen positions at a selected limit <=512 and
never truncates a population automatically. Broader fixed forecast coverage can
include inactive names whose constraints require exactly zero weight; their
zero variables can be omitted from the solve, with IDs/order retained explicitly
in the expanded target and risk checks. Every held name remains active/covered.
Larger/optimized execution remains
an extension requiring measured resources and parity. Zero targets, infeasible
constraints and missing evidence are distinct outcomes. Next-close drift or
partial fills can violate a decision-time target; batch admission checks that
outcome, and post-fill reports check actual retained positions every session.

Pin/check/register/run/inspect
-----------------------------
From the repository root, set $py to the environment Python and choose a NEW $out
directory, such as data/market/research/my-reviewed-study. The published example
is entirely synthetic, with deliberate capital, cost, borrow, timing and solver
settings; it validates integration rather than claiming alpha.

  & $py -m alpha_lab.research.cli study-pin `
      --packet examples/research/p3-study-packet.json --output "$out/inputs"
  & $py -m alpha_lab.research.cli study-spec --bundle "$out/inputs" `
      --request examples/research/p3-study-request.json --output "$out/spec.json"
  & $py -m alpha_lab.research.cli registry-init "$out/registry.sqlite"
  & $py -m alpha_lab.research.cli study-register --bundle "$out/inputs" `
      --spec "$out/spec.json" --registry "$out/registry.sqlite" `
      --family examples/research/p3-family.json --hypothesis examples/research/p3-hypothesis.json
  & $py -m alpha_lab.research.cli study-check --bundle "$out/inputs" --spec "$out/spec.json"
  & $py -m alpha_lab.research.cli study-run --bundle "$out/inputs" `
      --spec "$out/spec.json" --registry "$out/registry.sqlite" --output "$out/run"
  & $py -m alpha_lab.research.cli study-inspect "$out/run"

Use a single project registry for related actual research, with explicit parent
links and prior exposure declarations. The separate new registry in this example
is for the self-contained synthetic fixture, not a reset of research knowledge.
Study identities include the exact economic input packet and pinned forecast
files, code, environment (including SciPy), seed and threads. Names/resource-only
settings do not reset a trial. Family/attempt caps and explicit --retry-of use
the existing project ledger. No source/evaluator generation occurs in a job.

For equity-risk integration first run risk-pin --source <Forecast directory>
--request <explicit binding request JSON> --output <NEW pinned directory>.
Use risk-check with a complete RiskUse JSON. A study-pin --risk-sources JSON maps
context binding IDs to those pinned directories. Only these fixed job-facing
files are copied; full-history source-store paths are not passed to the worker.

study-from-signal --signal-run <verified P2 run> --development-bundle <its exact
P1 bundle> --template <raw-study template JSON> --output <NEW packet.json> bridges
saved validation scores and same-date beta into the ledger workflow. The template
must explicitly choose the raw-price cohort, sessions, costs and diagonal risk
baseline before compilation, with empty contexts, reconstructed history and PIT
attestation false. Missing source membership/score/beta remains unavailable;
future label coverage is not a filter. No performance-based winner/cohort is
chosen. P2 supplies no confirmed issuer/sector links, so the bridge requires
those optional constraints disabled; evidenced richer contexts can use the main
study API. Pin/register the compiled packet as a new linked methodology study.

The reference JSON input packet has a 64 MB pre-parse size cap. Risk arrays have
an independent 512 MB uncompressed cap. Only two validated immutable risk entries
are cached per worker; every use still checks information time and staleness,
and publication rehashes pinned inputs. These caps do not claim hard parent RAM
isolation or an optimized full-history feature/price store.

Study artifacts: spec.json, bundle.json, packet.json, run.json, ledger.json,
portfolio.json, runtime.json, report.txt and COMPLETE.json. Accounting and risk
failures remain reportable scientific outcomes. Zero process exit without exact
validated outputs is operational failure. Publication verifies identity, date
coverage, NAV reconciliation, source hashes and runtime controls before the
completion marker. A sampled tree RSS cap, wall deadline and output cap terminate
owned processes; these are not hard OS RAM limits or filesystem/network ACLs.
The parent also uses RAM for input validation. Job environments omit keys and
proxies. No API calls are required for normal studies.

P4 API, notebook and viewer
--------------------------
ResearchService(root) is the shared local interface for inspection, progress,
reports, explicit study execution/reconciliation and signal comparisons. The
CLI product commands and dashboard use it; notebooks/us_equity_research.ipynb
demonstrates the same read-only operations without auto-executed outputs.

  & $py -m alpha_lab.research.cli research-serve --root data/market/research
Then open http://127.0.0.1:8766/research. The existing viewer also exposes /research
with alpha-lab-serve --workspace <workspace> --research-root <saved library>.
Opening a page starts no experiment or LLM. The viewer has GET-only endpoints,
bounded library scans, safe library-relative IDs, verified report access and
plain-text rendering. Partial/corrupt artifacts have progress but no verified
report. There is no shell, write, trade, evaluator-generation or reveal route.
The default standalone listener is loopback; deliberately review any remote bind.

Reports link hypotheses/source identities, signal evidence, target and actual
positions, risk conventions, fills, costs, failures and assumptions. Runtime
status is separate from scientific acceptance. Heartbeats are recorded evidence,
not proof that a saved PID is still the same live process. The viewer does not
kill/restart jobs. After file/SQLite interruption, study-inspect and explicit
study-reconcile validate identity/path/hashes before updating the existing
attempt. Unknown outcomes block duplicates. Full automatic resume stays deferred.

Bounded public agent assistance
-------------------------------
PublicSignalTools exposes research_catalog, draft_recipe, validate_draft and
report_to_user. It can stage/validate fixed supported public recipes only. It
cannot read market data, coverage or performance; run tests; write evaluator
code; change budgets/splits; reveal holdout; or modify source attestations.
Draft validation is the allowed implementation task; local registration and
evaluation are separate explicit API/CLI actions. Renamed/rescaled duplicate
recipes do not consume another unique proposal. Fixed workers retire generic
evaluator generation from the standard equity path.

public-design --config <reviewed JSON> --output <NEW directory> is the only new
provider entry. It requires public_only, an explicit credential-free HTTPS base
URL matching local configuration, a one-to-four draft cap, every LLMSettings
field, an approved model, medium/high reasoning and future UTC deadline. The
review template intentionally leaves model/reasoning/deadline unset; it cannot
launch as supplied. No .env is read on imports, inspection or page loads.

Requests and any summaries share existing permanent LLM usage/intent controls,
call/token/time caps and serialization. Runs record exact public prompts/tool
schemas, source/runtime fingerprints and times. SDK/automatic retries are zero;
failures and cleanup errors preserve failed intent rather than reporting completion.
Temperature/top_p are intentionally omitted, store=false is requested, and
gateway retention/pricing remain unknown. Output controls and tool authority are
application restrictions, not OS security isolation. No LLM campaign or private
Alva data flow is authorized merely by viewing a config or dashboard.

External evidence and unresolved data
-------------------------------------
PortfolioPolicy optionally accepts target_limits with max_gross, max_name,
max_turnover, net_tolerance and beta_tolerance. Every target bound must be no
looser than its corresponding hard policy bound. Only score projection uses
these stricter construction limits; next-close fill admission and daily retained
position checks continue to use the original hard policy. Plans record both
sets. Failed construction has no unbuffered fallback. Explicit zero net/beta
construction tolerances center the intended book; they do not guarantee that
later price gaps or costs stay within hard bounds. Frozen held positions can
make buffered construction infeasible. Missing target_limits preserves earlier
policy serialization and behavior. Buffers enter the bundle/computation identity;
changing them after a completed trial requires a newly registered linked trial.

Real-source exploratory ledgers may mark cash-dividend inputs as
declared_dividend_assumption when historical unit conversion or payment timing
is a declared research convention. The evidence hash must identify the source
and assumption; the journal retains the label. This label is limited to cash
dividends, cannot supply merger/distribution terms, and cannot enter a scenario
claiming point-in-time attestation. It does not establish observed settlement or
resolve a stock-spinoff entitlement. Split-adjusted dividend/volume records must
be put on the ledger's raw-share basis without also earning adjusted returns.

evidence-audit imports explicitly chosen historical work queues and a transition
clarification packet into a new local hashed worklist. Original records and
requirements remain unchanged. It adds the historical broker-lending requirement.
evidence-attach preserves a bounded primary document in a new attachment and
marks it for human review; it cannot close a case or change an attestation.

The existing 131 economic/PIT cases and 21 vendor-transition clarifications
require source evidence. Historical borrower capacity/rates/recalls, action
entitlements/payment/delivery, dated issuer/eligibility and feed arrival/revisions
cannot be obtained by changing an interface or inventing a backtest value.
Keep stronger historical/executable claims blocked until those records are
reviewed. Sharadar remains first, Tiingo retains established gaps, and Arrays
serves only previously uncovered US equity feeds. This milestone changes no
provider priority and launches no new data subscription or vendor API campaign.

Acceptance evidence and rollback
--------------------------------
Hand-derived tests cover long/short cash, round-trip signed fees/slippage,
splits, dividend entitlement/payables/payment, weekend cash/funding/borrow,
renames, cash/stock/mixed mergers, delayed/unmarked consideration, partial/failed
fills, stale recovery, expired/recall loan observations and censored pending
orders. Synthetic adapter/projection/integration tests reject semantic mismatch,
missing evidence, infeasibility and future inputs. Native worker and API tests
exercise publication gaps, duplicate prevention, read-only behavior and public
tool boundaries. These demonstrate scoped software behavior, not economic alpha.

Keep immutable inputs/artifacts/registry attempts when rolling back. P1/P2 CLI
commands and archived inspectors remain usable. Existing legacy behavior and the
original frozen selection/holdout/backtester are preserved. The sibling equity-
risk project is consumed read-only. Disable the research router/optional agent
tools without affecting deterministic CLI correctness or saved evidence.

Explicitly deferred extensions remain deferred: automatic campaign resume,
richer ML/text alpha (including pretrained-model historical admissibility),
optimized large-universe execution, intraday/live brokerage, broad package
restructuring and multi-agent scale-up. They have separate prerequisites and
were not silently included by the P3/P4 engineering work.

Stock-distribution and daily-control extension
----------------------------------------------
Action kind stock_distribution retains the parent and books signed recipient
shares: a short parent creates a short recipient obligation, not cash income.
The current primitive explicitly requires distribution_convention
signed_fractional_prior_close_same_session_delivery_research and same-session
payment_date. Real-source scenarios must label this
declared_distribution_assumption; it cannot establish PIT attestation or verified
broker settlement. Fractional cash-in-lieu, taxes and actual loan delivery are
not reconstructed. A vendor valuation companion is not another cash dividend.
Use raw recipient marks and include received shares in risk, dividends, borrow
and final disposition. Missing recipient marks still suppress observed NAV.

StudyPacket optionally accepts daily_risk_controls, methodology
close_breach_uniform_reduction_v1, fill_lag_sessions=1, inner max_gross/max_name/
net_tolerance/beta_tolerance, and exit_ineligible_holdings. Inner limits cannot
exceed hard portfolio limits. Existing packets omit this extension when unset.
Daily close observations use current NAV/quotes and the latest known context.
Hard breaches trigger uniform reductions of held shares; optionally exit held
non-alpha receipts. No new positions or score reranking occur in this path.
Required reductions larger than hard turnover are staged at 90% of that cap.
Fixed quantities fill next session with normal costs, raw-volume participation,
hard admission checks and reduce-only treatment of pre-existing violations.
An intervention is not a guarantee of restored exposure: gaps, partial/no fills
and unavailable evidence remain visible in risk_controls/actual_position_risk.
Without observed NAV/coverage, sizing is blocked and reported. Last-session
triggers are right-censored, never executed at the same close.

Daily reductions override scheduled alpha increases. An explicitly declared
PortfolioContext intent=close_all, with all signals absent/ineligible, takes
priority and submits exact zero-share targets for held assets, including received
securities. This avoids optimizer numerical residue in a predeclared liquidation.
Unheld ineligible zero targets do not pre-order future distributions when daily
controls are enabled. Add received assets to dated context/risk coverage with no
alpha score or eligibility. Missing held-asset coverage is never silently dropped.

Freeze the control/event conventions and data availability assumptions before a
new linked registered trial. Preserve preceding registries/results and reported
failures. Accounting/control changes do not create fresh alpha validation.
